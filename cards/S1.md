# 引句卡：S1 Cahill et al. 2013（Breakfast Eating and Incident Coronary Heart Disease）

> 格式見 [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) 第 5.1 節。頁面中的英文引句只能取自這裡。

## C1-01
- 原文："Eating habits including breakfast eating were assessed in 1992 in 26,902 American men aged 45-82 years from the Health Professionals Follow-up Study who were free of cardiovascular disease and cancer."
- 章節：Abstract
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：研究在 1992 年調查了 26,902 名 45–82 歲、沒有心血管疾病和癌症的美國男性（來自醫療專業人員追蹤研究）的飲食習慣，包括是否吃早餐。

## C1-02
- 原文："During 16 years of follow-up, 1,527 incident CHD cases were diagnosed."
- 章節：Abstract
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：追蹤 16 年期間，共有 1,527 人新診斷出冠心病。

## C1-03
- 原文："Men who skipped breakfast had a 27% higher risk of CHD as compared with men who did not (RR=1.27, 95% CI:1.06-1.53)."
- 章節：Abstract
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：as compared with men who did not
- 中文：比起吃早餐的男性，不吃早餐的男性冠心病風險高 27%（RR=1.27，95% CI 1.06–1.53）。

## C1-04
- 原文："In age-adjusted models, men who did not eat breakfast had a 33% higher risk of CHD as compared with men who did (RR=1.33, 95% CI: 1.13-1.57)"
- 章節：Results
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：as compared with men who did
- 中文：只調整年齡時，比起吃早餐的男性，不吃早餐的男性冠心病風險高 33%（RR=1.33，95% CI 1.13–1.57）。

## C1-05
- 原文："but was attenuated (RR=1.18, 95% CI: 0.98-1.43) when further adjusted for the potential mediators of BMI, hypercholesterolemia, hypertension and diabetes"
- 章節：Results
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：再進一步調整 BMI、高膽固醇、高血壓和糖尿病這些可能的中介因子後，風險減弱為 RR=1.18（95% CI 0.98–1.43）。

## C1-06
- 原文："Participants who did not report eating breakfast were younger than those who did, and were more likely to be smokers, employed full time, unmarried, less physically active, and drink more alcohol"
- 章節：Results
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：than those who did
- 中文：比起有吃早餐的人，沒吃早餐的參與者比較年輕，也比較可能抽菸、全職工作、未婚、運動較少、喝較多酒。

## C1-07
- 原文："Men who ate breakfast ate on average one more time per day than those who skipped breakfast, implying that those who abstained from breakfast were not eating additional make-up meals later in the day."
- 章節：Results
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：than those who skipped breakfast
- 中文：吃早餐的男性平均每天比不吃早餐的男性多吃一次；作者據此推論，不吃早餐的人並沒有在當天稍晚額外補吃。

## C1-08
- 原文："among men ≤ 60 years old, those who skipped breakfast had a 50% higher risk of CHD as compared with men who ate breakfast (multivariate RR = 1.55, 95% CI: 1.09-2.22)"
- 章節：Results
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：as compared with men who ate breakfast
- 中文：在 60 歲以下的男性中，比起吃早餐的人，不吃早餐的人冠心病風險較高：文字寫高 50%，但同一句的 RR 為 1.55（95% CI 1.09–2.22）。

## C1-09
- 原文："while this association was not significant in the older half of participants (RR = 1.06, 95%CI: 0.84-1.33"
- 章節：Results
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：在年紀較大的那一半參與者中，這個關聯並不顯著（RR=1.06，95% CI 0.84–1.33）。

## C1-10
- 原文："Compared with men who did not eat late at night, those who ate late at night had a 55% higher CHD risk (RR=1.55, 95% CI:1.05-2.29)."
- 章節：Abstract
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：Compared with men who did not eat late at night
- 中文：比起不吃宵夜的男性，睡覺後還吃東西的男性冠心病風險高 55%（RR=1.55，95% CI 1.05–2.29）。

## C1-11
- 原文："No association was observed between eating frequency (times/day) and risk of CHD."
- 章節：Abstract
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：一天吃幾次與冠心病風險之間，沒有觀察到關聯。

## C1-12
- 原文："Eating breakfast was associated with significantly lower CHD risk in this cohort of male health professionals."
- 章節：Abstract, Conclusions
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：在這群男性醫療專業人員中，吃早餐與顯著較低的冠心病風險相關。

## C1-13
- 原文："the associations we report are significant but modest, requiring replication"
- 章節：Discussion
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：作者表示，他們發現的關聯雖然顯著，但幅度不大，需要其他研究重現。

## C1-14
- 原文："Although it is commonly stated that breakfast is the most important meal of the day, there exist no evidence-based recommendations for adults regarding eating habits"
- 章節：Introduction
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：雖然常有人說早餐是一天最重要的一餐，但針對成人的進食習慣，並沒有以證據為基礎的建議。

## C1-15
- 原文："The 2010 Dietary Guidelines for Americans recommend breakfast for children, but make no recommendation for adults"
- 章節：Introduction
- 主詞：S1 轉述（2010 美國飲食指南）
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：S1 轉述，2010 年美國飲食指南建議兒童吃早餐，但對成人沒有提出建議。

## C1-16
- 原文："It has been previously reported in the HPFS that compared to men who ate breakfast, participants who skipped breakfast were 15% more likely to have substantial weight gain"
- 章節：Discussion
- 主詞：S1 轉述（同一世代的先前研究）
- 比較對象：compared to men who ate breakfast
- 中文：S1 轉述同一世代的先前研究：比起吃早餐的男性，不吃早餐的人體重大幅增加的可能性高 15%。

## C1-17
- 原文："Although we adjusted for factors such as diet quality, stress and having regular physical exams to control for a chaotic versus stable lifestyle, it is possible that eating habits could be a marker of lifestyle consistency or general health-seeking behavior."
- 章節：Discussion
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：作者指出，雖然已調整飲食品質、壓力、定期健檢等因素，飲食習慣仍有可能只是生活規律或整體重視健康的標記。

## C1-18

- 章節：Discussion
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：作者表示，他們無法排除有未測量到的干擾因子的可能性。

## C1-19

- 章節：Methods
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：約 97% 的參與者是歐洲白人後裔。

## C1-20
- 原文："The overall average diet quality of our study population was good, so it is not known if our results are generalizable to other populations with lower diet quality."

## C1-21

- 章節：Discussion
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無

## C1-22

- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：作者認為，如果在女性和其他族群中得到重現，這項研究的結果可以作為醫師和衛生單位提出每天吃早餐建議的證據。

## C1-23

- 比較對象：無

## C1-24

- 比較對象：無

## C1-25
- 原文："we defined breakfast as a positive response to any of the first three eating times"
- 章節：Methods
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：只要參與者在前三個進食時段（早餐前、早餐、早餐與午餐之間）中任一個回答有吃，就算有吃早餐。

## C1-26
- 原文："Multivariate models were adjusted for known and suspected risk factors of CHD such as energy intake"
- 章節：Methods
- 主詞：S1 作者
- 比較對象：無
- 中文：多變項模型調整了已知與疑似的冠心病風險因子，例如熱量攝取。

## C1-27

## C1-28
- 原文："The present study only had data on regular eating habits, and so we could not estimate the risk of CHD associated with occasional eating habits such as skipping breakfast a couple of days a week."

- 中文：研究只有固定飲食習慣的資料，所以無法估計與偶爾不吃早餐（例如一週只有幾天不吃）相關的冠心病風險可能有多大。
