# Sievert 2019：吃早餐對體重與熱量的影響（統合分析）

> **一句話重點**：綜合 13 個隨機對照試驗，吃早餐的人體重平均略重 0.44 公斤、每天多吃約 260 大卡（各試驗熱量結果相當不一致）；作者認為增加早餐可能不是好的減重策略，但納入研究品質偏低，結果要謹慎解讀。

## 白話說明

單一實驗可能剛好人太少或時間太短。統合分析就是把很多個實驗的結果「合起來算」，看整體趨勢（本 wiki 的教學說明）。S5 收集了比較「吃早餐」和「不吃早餐」的隨機對照試驗，合併分析體重和熱量（S5）。

- 共納入 13 個試驗：7 個看體重變化，10 個看熱量攝取（S5）。
  > "Of 13 included trials, seven examined the effect of eating breakfast on weight change, and 10 examined the effect on energy intake." — S5, Abstract

## 主要發現

- **體重**：統合分析發現體重有小差異，對不吃早餐的人較有利，吃早餐的人平均重 0.44 公斤；但各試驗結果有些不一致（S5）。
  > "Meta-analysis of the results found a small difference in weight favouring participants who skipped breakfast (mean difference 0.44 kg, 95% confidence interval 0.07 to 0.82), but there was some inconsistency across trial results" — S5, Abstract
- **熱量**：比起被分到不吃早餐的人，被分到吃早餐的人每天總熱量攝取較高，平均多 259.79 大卡（S5）。
  > "Participants assigned to breakfast had a higher total daily energy intake than those assigned to skip breakfast (mean difference 259.79 kcal/day, 78.87 to 440.71" — S5, Abstract
  - 不過各試驗的熱量結果有相當大的不一致（I²=80%）（S5）。
    > "despite substantial inconsistency across trial results (I 2 =80%)" — S5, Abstract
- 作者指出，沒有證據支持「吃早餐促進減重」或「不吃早餐讓人變胖」的說法（S5）。
  > "found no evidence to support the notion that breakfast consumption promotes weight loss or that skipping breakfast leads to weight gain" — S5, Discussion
- **作者結論**：作者認為，這項研究暗示：不論原本有沒有吃早餐的習慣，增加早餐可能都不是好的減重策略（S5）。
  > "This study suggests that the addition of breakfast might not be a good strategy for weight loss, regardless of established breakfast habit." — S5, Abstract, Conclusion
- 作者提醒，為了減重而建議成人吃早餐要謹慎，因為可能有反效果（S5）。這是作者對研究者與衛生單位的提醒，本 wiki 不把它當成給讀者的建議。
  > "Caution is needed when recommending breakfast for weight loss in adults, as it could have the opposite effect." — S5, Abstract, Conclusion

## 為什麼實驗和觀察型研究結果不同？

- 作者認為，隨機對照試驗和觀察型研究結果不一致，很可能反映了社經因素和健康生活型態造成的殘餘干擾（S5），見[干擾因子](../concepts/confounding.md)。
  > "the discordance between findings from the randomised controlled trials and observational studies are likely to reflect residual confounding by socioeconomic factors and healthy lifestyles" — S5, Discussion

## 不吃早餐的人會補吃嗎？活動量呢？

- 作者綜合認為，資料不支持「不吃早餐可能讓人當天稍晚吃進過多熱量」這個假設（S5）。見[爭議頁](../debates/do-skippers-eat-more-later.md)。
  > "Taken together, the data do not support the assumption that omitting breakfast might lead to overconsumption of calories later in the day." — S5, Discussion
- 活動量的結果分歧：有 3 個研究發現，吃與不吃早餐的人身體活動量沒有顯著差異（S5）。
  > "Three studies reported no significant difference in physical activity between breakfast consumers and breakfast non-consumers." — S5, Discussion
- 另有 2 個研究發現，吃早餐與身體活動量增加相關，尤其是在早上（S5）。
  > "Two studies found that breakfast consumption was associated with increased physical activity, particularly during the morning." — S5, Discussion
- 但作者總結，比起不吃早餐的人，吃早餐的人每天身體活動的總消耗並沒有顯著較高（S5）。這和 S4 單一試驗發現的「活動消耗明顯較高」不同，見 [Betts 2014](betts-2014.md)（本 wiki 並列呈現，不判斷誰對）。
  > "However, total thermogenesis from daily physical activity was not significantly higher in breakfast consumers than in breakfast non-consumers." — S5, Discussion

## 研究限制（作者自己提到的）

- 所有納入的試驗至少有一個面向的偏誤風險高或不明，而且追蹤期都很短（體重平均 7 週，熱量平均 2 週）（S5）。
  > "All of the included trials were at high or unclear risk of bias in at least one domain and had only short term follow-ups (mean period seven weeks for weight, two weeks for energy intake)." — S5, Abstract
- 因為納入研究的品質大多偏低，結果要謹慎解讀（S5）。
  > "As the quality of the included studies was mostly low, the findings should be interpreted with caution." — S5, Abstract
- 作者表示，還需要更多高品質的隨機對照試驗（S5）。
  > "Further randomised controlled trials of high quality are needed" — S5, Abstract, Conclusion
